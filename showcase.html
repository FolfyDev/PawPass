Six things a con or meetup usually cobbles together from separate tools, running instead as one system that already knows about every attendee.

REG
Registration, two ways in

Sign up on the website or entirely inside Telegram — same event, same waitlist, same badge on the other end.

No account needed
BDG
Badges & wallet passes

A QR-coded badge rendered from a layout you design, plus Apple/Google Wallet passes attendees add with one tap.

Print & digital
KSK
Kiosk mode

A walk-up desk for the day of — take a name, log the cash or card, print the badge, reset for the next person.

At the door
MER
Merch & donations

Stock counts that can't oversell, a sales log per item, and a spot to record cash donations that aren't tied to a purchase.

Table-side
VCH
Voucher codes

Hand out a code for "Organizer" or "Photographer" — it prints on the badge and skips the line, capacity included.

Custom badge tiers
CSH
Cash reconciliation

Door donations and table sales land in one ledger, broken out by payment method, exportable at the end of the night.

Reconciled

One identity, two doors

Telegram or the web — it's the same ticket

Most attendees register from chat without ever loading a page. Anyone who'd rather not touch Telegram at all can register straight from the browser instead, no account required up front.

Via Telegram

/register, /mytickets, /rsvp

The bot walks through name, custom questions, and terms, then hands back a QR code and a badge number right in the chat — no separate login step.

Via the web

Guest checkout, real account

Fill out the form, get registered, and set a password afterward if you want back in later. Telegram can still be linked from the account page any time.

Behind the badge

Built for whoever's standing at the door

A camera-based scanner for check-in and reprints, an audit log of who did what, staff broadcast messages sent straight to Telegram, and role-gated admin access — the parts that matter once the event is actually running.

Self-hosted

Your event, your database

One docker compose up away: Postgres for the data, a Node API, a React front end, and a Telegram bot, all running on infrastructure you control. No per-attendee fee, no third party holding your registrant list.
